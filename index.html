GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Here are a few things patients often ask before visiting.

How do I book an appointment? +

Send us an appointment inquiry using the form below, or contact the clinic through your preferred messaging channel.

Do I need to prepare anything for my first visit? +

Bring any relevant dental records, a list of medications, and questions you’d like to discuss. The clinic can advise if anything else is needed.

Do you accept walk-ins? +

Availability can vary. Please contact the clinic first to confirm whether a walk-in visit can be accommodated.

Can I ask about treatment costs before booking? +

Of course. Send us your concern and we can let you know what information is available before your consultation.
